Centrally-Managed Graders

Jump to top

This course uses centrally-managed, certified graders for all grading. The graders are managed by the Online Learning Grading team. ⭐ Please speak kindly of the graders to the students. Communication paths regarding graders and the grading team are outlined in the section of your OL 110 WWB Instructor Certification labeled "Be a Team Player."

Things you need to know about centralized grading:

  • ❌ Instructors should not adjust grades in the gradebook. It is expected that instructors support the grades and feedback awarded. If there is a grade you disagree with, please contact the ACL (or your supervisor if you are an international instructor) to discuss this issue.
  • The Grading Queue prioritizes student submissions first according to the due date, and then batches like assignments together from the oldest to the newest submissions for maximum efficiency. This is a modified FIFO (first-in, first-out) order without respect to the course section. This means that you will have a variety of graders grading your students' work. Graders strive to grade work as soon as possible, but generally within 72 hours of the submission time after the due date or earlier. 
  • Grades in this course will be posted automatically. Do not adjust the Gradebook Settings.
  • Resubmissions: Students may resubmit assignments 1–2 times according to what assignment settings allow. This allows students who mistakenly submit the wrong assignment to resubmit the correct assignment. Instructors can give students additional submissions if needed. Be aware that instructors cannot adjust that setting for only one student. If instructors allow additional submissions, they allow them for the whole class. That is usually not an issue because the other students don’t notice the change, but the student who needs additional submissions is able to turn in their assignment again.
  • Academic Dishonesty: The graders will note any potential plagiarisms or AI issues and bring them to the attention of the Instructor. It is the instructors responsibility to determine if plagiarism/AI use has taken place, address it with the student and adjust the students grade if necessary. 

    Graders will not note suspected plagiarism/AI use in Turnitin. Graders are to grade the assignment as if there is no suspicion of plagiarism/AI. All decisions and actions toward the student regarding plagiarism or AI use come from the instructors.
    Cheating and plagiarism situations are one of the rare exceptions to instructors changing grades in the gradebook when they have centralized graders. If you have any questions about how to best handle suspected plagiarism, contact your ACL (or your supervisor if you are an international instructor).
