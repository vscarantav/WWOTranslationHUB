BYU-Pathway Worldwide, BYU-Idaho, and Ensign College work together to meet the needs of online students, like yourself, in all parts of the world!

 

 

Think of BYU-Pathway Worldwide similar to a “gateway entrance”.

As you first enter the gate, you immediately find PathwayConnect,

to get you started on your educational journey. You are a BYU-Pathway Worldwide student.

 

 

Once you have completed PathwayConnect (PC101, PC102, & PC103) and your first certificate course, you "matriculate " and become a BYU-Idaho or Ensign College student. You will continue using the BYU-Pathway web portal for registration and student services, but you are on track to earn certificates and degrees from BYU-Idaho or Ensign College.

 

 

Degrees are awarded by BYU-Idaho and Ensign College,
while BYU-Pathway provides the resources to help you succeed!

 

 

 

Your classes and instructors are provided by
BYU-Idaho and Ensign College.

 

 

 

BYU-Pathway provides services such as

class registration, tuition payment, technical support, advising, tutoring, and more!

Go to your student portal to learn more.
