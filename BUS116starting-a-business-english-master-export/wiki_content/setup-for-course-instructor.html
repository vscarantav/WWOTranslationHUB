Course-Specific Setup for Instructor

Setup WhatsApp

It is important that these steps are completed before students have access to the course.

This video shows how to do the steps below: WhatsApp Setup for Entrepreneurship Certificate.

  1. Access your WhatsApp Group Survey and the corresponding spreadsheet.
    • Make sure you only use the one with your name on it. If there isn't a survey or spreadsheet with your name on it, please reach out to your ACL.
    • In the response spreadsheet, copy the student data into a new tab, then delete the rows, so the spreadsheet is ready for the new term. You don't need to do this step if this is the first time you have used this survey.
  2. Add the link to the survey in two places: 
    1. In the email below that you send out to your students
    2. In the student's Canvas module/page: W01 Start Here
  3. Setup your WhatsApp groups. You can name your groups A1, B1, C1, and D1 based on where students live. But it's flexible; you can organize them however you think is best. Just remember, in the next class, students will be asked which group they were in, so it's easier to keep them with the same people. It's good to stick to the naming convention if you can.
    1. A1 - Africa and Europe
    2. B1 - Asia, Australia, Philippines, and Pacific
    3. C1 - North America West
    4. D1 - South America and North America East
  4. Add students to your groups as they complete the survey.
    1. Instead of adding students one by one, send a bcc group email to the email they gave in the survey or email via Canvas.
    2. Most students will join without issue. For the few who were late to complete the survey, send them a direct WhatsApp message inviting them to join.
  5. Recommendation: Creating groups in Canvas that correspond to the Groups in WhatsApp. The Canvas groups allow easy access to Student's activity and completion of assignments. If needed it is where comments can be initiated for underperformance.

Welcome Email Instructions

Please send a welcome email to your students as soon as you have access to your class roll (likely the weekend before the semester begins) to establish expectations and your desired learning environment. Your email should be welcoming, inviting, and informative. Please include the following in your email:

  • The date class begins and ends and how to get started in the course:
    • Invite students to access the course promptly.
    • Inform students they will need to complete assignments according to the set due dates. 
  • An invitation and instructions on how to access the course.
  • A list of text and/or software for the class; let them know if course material will be contained in the course.
  • Any unique course characteristics.
  • An invitation to have students update their profile and notifications.
  • How students can contact you:
    • Provide your email address and, if you would like, a phone number. 
  • The expectation that students will check the announcements section of the homepage each time they log into their course.

Personalize your welcome email with additional information as desired. For more help with your welcome email, go to: Pre-term Preparation: Sending a Welcome Message to Your Students.
