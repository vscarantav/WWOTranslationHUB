Q: Why isn't there anyone in my WhatsApp group?

A: When you join WhatsApp you can't see any of the previous posts. So when someone joins your group, say "hi" to them. Others will join eventually. The instructor will post the welcome announcement a few times during the week, so that everyone can see it.

Q: Is there a gathering for this class?

A: No. In this class we have students from all over the world, so we can't meet in person. The connections and friendships we will make in this class will be through our WhatsApp groups. Get to know the people in your WhatsApp group. You will be giving each other feedback and cheering each other along during this class.

Q: How do I contact my instructor?

A: You'll see your instructor show up in your WhatsApp group which is the best way to contact your instructor. You can also send a private message or email your instructor directly. 

Q: Are we using the Canvas Inbox in this class for communication?

A: You can contact your instructor through the Canvas Inbox, email, or through WhatsApp. You will get messages from your instructor through the Canvas Inbox.

Q: Do we use Canvas in this class?

A: The course material is in the BUS 116 Workbook. The workbook will tell you when it's time to come back to Canvas and submit an assignment.

Q: What if I'm not ready to start a business?

A: You will need to start a business in this course but that doesn't mean it needs to be big or expensive. In fact, we're going to debunk the myth that you need a lot of money to start a business. This is course is the first of four courses designed to help you become a successful entrepreneur. 

  1. BUS 116 Starting a Business
  2. BUS 117 Growing a Business
  3. BUS 118 Expanding a Business
  4. BUS 119 Strengthening a Business

This course will guide you through the process of identifying business opportunities and understanding your customer. You'll have the opportunity to explore being an entrepreneur with the support of the course and your peers—so jump in you don't want to miss this opportunity!

Q: Does my business have to make money during this course?

A: Yes. You should have sales by the end of the course. Keep in mind that selling a prototype of your product or service or pre-sales is a great way to test your idea before your business is ready to launch. You can also sell something that will help you to earn the capital you need to build a business you eventually want to start.
