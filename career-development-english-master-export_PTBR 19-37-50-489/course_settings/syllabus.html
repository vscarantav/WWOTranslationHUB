Overview

In this course, students develop resources, professional connections and essential employability skills to obtain or improve employment in a field related to their university certificate. Students will network and apply for job opportunities in their industry. The life-long skills gained in this course are valuable for both active and future job seekers.

Structure

Course Outcomes

  1. Communicate personal and essential employable skills matched to a target job.
  2. Create a list of employers, contacts and opportunities to obtain or improve employment.
  3. Prepare effective professional documents and digital profile that incorporate essential employability skills.
  4. Identify and practice effective networking and job-search strategies.
  5. Connect and network with professionals, mentors and potential employers in student's target industry.
  6. Apply for jobs and/or improved position in current organization.
  7. Demonstrate effective interviewing and post-interviewing skills.

As with all of your other courses at BYU-Pathway, you can develop intellectually and spiritually throughout this course. This course follows the BYU-Pathway Learning Model in the following manner:

  • Teach One Another: Meaningfully participate in weekly Share discussions through WhatsApp.

You can expect to receive grades and feedback within seven days of the due date for all assignments.

Group Work

WhatsApp

In Week 1, you will be put into a WhatsApp Group. This group is for you to work with your classmates on assignments. You will also be added to a WhatsApp communication channel where the instructor can post announcements. Remember these groups and the information in these groups (including WhatsApp contact information) is only to be used for class purposes.

Late Work

Turning work in on time is a sign of professionalism and respect for the instructor as well as the other students in the class. Students are expected to submit all work on time.

Policy Details
Due Date Assignments are due in the middle and the end of the week. See the modules page for exact due dates and time.
Late Work

If you miss the deadline for an assignment or graded quiz, it will receive a zero score automatically.

However, you may submit late work within two weeks of the due date (or, for Weeks 5–7, by the end of the term) to receive credit. To avoid falling behind, it is best to submit your work on time or early.
Resubmission

Resubmissions are allowed in this class, so you can improve your score. However, the resubmission must be submitted within two weeks of the due date (or, for Weeks 5–7, by the end of the term).
