Overview

  • Purpose: Team learning is a collaborative effort and teams are an essential part of most career fields. Working in groups supports the principles of love, service, and teaching one another.
  • Task: Sign up for a group in Canvas based on your preferred time and peer interactions and start connecting with other students and your group in MS Teams.
During this course, you are required to meet with your group synchronously at least once each week and collaborate with your group asynchronously throughout the week.

Instructions

  1. Complete the previous activity, W01 Setup: Development Environment, in which you will install Microsoft Teams and connect with course members.
  2. Go to the People tab and select a group based upon your preferred meeting time.
    1. Select the WDD330 Team tab.
    2. Self-select one of the groups based upon your preferred weekly meeting time.
  3. In the Microsoft Teams application, find the channel that corresponds to the group you selected in Canvas. It may be listed among the Hidden Channels at the bottom of the list.
  4. Select Show for that group channel.
  5. In your Microsoft Teams group channel, reach out right and introduce yourself, and connect with your group members.
  6. Monitor the channel for additional members throughout the week.
  7. In Microsoft Teams, turn on notifications for the General channel and your group's channel.
  8. Once you have set up everything, posted, and connected with a team member, mark this module as complete here in Canvas.
