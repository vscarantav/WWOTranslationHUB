All course materials are in the workbook. This course uses a tool called the Learning Library where you will access the course workbook. The workbook contains all the information, learning material, directions, assignments, rubrics, and grades you need to complete this course.

Download the Workbook

  1. Go to https://study.byupathway.edu in a web browser (not the Canvas app)
  2. Click Sign In.
  3. Use your Church Account username and password to sign in.
  4. Click the + button (Add Materials) at the bottom right of the page.
  5. Click on Entrepreneurship.
  6. Download the workbook for this course.

If you have problems accessing the Workbook, contact your instructor in WhatsApp or in the Canvas Inbox.
