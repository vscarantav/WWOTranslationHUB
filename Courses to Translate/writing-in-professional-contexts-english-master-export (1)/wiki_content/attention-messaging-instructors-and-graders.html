Students who have questions about assignment scoring or grades should contact the grading team through the "Add a Comment" box when working in their assignment submission area.

Graders are unable to answer questions sent to "Teaching Assistants" through the course Inbox or Communications tab, and this course does not use Teaching Assistants (TAs).

Students should use the Inbox feature to communicate with the instructor about personal issues or other questions about the course or course content. They can communicate with the instructor by choosing the "Teachers" option from the drop-down "To" list when composing a message in the Inbox.
