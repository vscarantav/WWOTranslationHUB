Chapter 4 Draft: Interview Summary

Overview

  • Task : You will conduct a 30–45 minute interview with a family member or close family friend. You will gather information about your family's history, traditions, or experiences. Then you will write a narrative summary demonstrating mastery of three source integration techniques from Chapter 4: attribution introduction, mid-sentence integration, and paraphrase with supporting quote.
  • Purpose: To practice integrating sources into your writing while clearly presenting and supporting ideas from an interview.
  • Length : 350–400 words

Instructions

Step 1: Prepare

Select your interview subject and develop 5–7 open-ended questions. Focus on family history, traditions, challenges, or cultural background. Contact your subject at least 48 hours in advance. Choose someone who enjoys sharing stories: parents, grandparents, elderly relatives, or close family friends. If family members are not available, consider family friends, neighbors, or community elders who know your family. Focus on questions that encourage storytelling:

  • "Tell me about a time when our family faced a significant challenge. How did you or they handle it?"
  • "What family traditions were important when you were growing up?"
  • "How did our family come to live in [location]? What was that experience like?"
  • "What values or lessons did your parents emphasize?"

Step 2: Interview

Conduct your interview, taking detailed notes and recording exact quotes you want to use. Focus on gathering specific stories and details. Take notes on both direct quotes you might use and longer responses you will paraphrase. Phone or video calls work well if distance is an issue.

Step 3: Organize

Review your notes and identify main themes. Select quotes for direct citation and information for paraphrasing. Group related information thematically rather than chronologically.

Step 4: Write

Compose your narrative summary of what stood out in your interviews

  • Attribution Introduction: According to [Name], "direct quote," which demonstrates [your analysis]
  • Mid-Sentence Integration: Your analysis when [Name] explained that "direct quote" during [context]
  • Paraphrase with Supporting Quote: [Name] paraphrased main point, stating "supporting direct quote"

Step 5: Cite

Include proper APA citation for your interview source. Add any attribution statements if AI assistance was used. Save file as "w04_family_interview-FirstName_LastName" and submit as Word document (.doc or .docx) or PDF. Use APA format: LastName, F. (Year, Month Day). Personal interview.

One important note on AI and citation formatting:

AI tools can format APA citations quickly, and you may use an AI tool to help format your interview citation after you have gathered all the source details yourself. Before you submit, verify the formatted citation against the actual information you recorded, the person's name, the date of the interview, and the format requirements from your program's APA Style Guide. AI-generated citations sometimes contain errors in punctuation, capitalization, or format even when the source information is correct.

Verifying the citation is your responsibility.

Do not enter your interviewee's full name, contact information, or personal details into an AI tool. Use initials or a description such as "family member" if you need AI help with citation format.

Step 6: Requirements Checklist

Before you submit, verify:

  • Conducted genuine interview with family member, family friend, or community elder (minimum 30 minutes)
  • Used all three source integration techniques from Chapter 4
  • Included at least one properly formatted APA interview citation
  • Word count between 350–400 words in narrative format Connected findings to your Personal History Project goals

AI Usage Policy

This assignment develops primary source research skills through authentic family interviews and personal connection to your family history. AI cannot conduct interviews, know your family's unique stories, or understand your personal relationship to your heritage. The learning objectives focus on building genuine interviewing techniques and source integration skills that only come from real human interactions and your own analysis of family information.

PROHIBITED : Having AI write content, ideas, or analysis for you

  • Why prohibited: AI cannot conduct interviews, generate authentic family stories, or provide genuine insights about your family connections and heritage

ASK FIRST : Translation assistance or major editing questions

  • Why restricted: Translation support should preserve your family's original meaning and cultural context, and major edits should maintain your voice

APPROVED : Grammar and spelling identification after completing your draft

  • Why allowed: Technical writing support does not compromise your authentic family content and helps all students communicate their stories clearly
Approved AI Prompts

Use these prompts to stay in control of your work:

  • Grammar Check : "Please identify grammar and spelling errors in this paragraph without changing my ideas: [paste your text]"
  • Clarity Review : "Which sentences in this paragraph could be clearer? Please point them out without rewriting: [paste your text]"
  • Translation Support : "Help me express this family story more clearly in English while keeping the original meaning and cultural context: [paste specific sentence]"
