Chapter 6 Writing Project: Family History Revision

Overview

  • Task : You will revise your Week 4 Family History assignment using the three-stage revision process: big picture content and organization changes, paragraph-level improvements, and close-up editing for style and mechanics. You will then write a 250-word reflection documenting your revision decisions and learning insights.
  • Purpose: To strengthen your writing through revision and reflect on how purposeful changes improve clarity, organization, and effectiveness.
  • Length : Original essay length plus 250-word reflection

Instructions

Step 1: Analyze

Read your Week 4 Family History completely and review your WriteGrader feedback (your automated writing feedback from the Week 5 submission). Identify big picture issues: Does your thesis clearly state your main argument? Do paragraphs build logically toward your conclusion? Do you adequately support each main point? Does your essay accomplish what you intended?

Step 2: Restructure

Make major content and organizational changes. Strengthen your thesis, rearrange paragraphs for better flow, add or remove evidence, and improve your introduction and conclusion. Focus on argument strength, logical organization, and adequate evidence support.

Step 3: Refine

Focus on paragraph-level improvements. Confirm that each paragraph focuses on one main idea with sufficient development. Add specific details and examples, create smooth transitions between paragraphs, and verify every paragraph directly supports your thesis.

Step 4: Polish

Complete close-up editing for sentence variety, precise word choice, grammar, and mechanics. Vary sentence structures, replace vague terms with specific language, and maintain academic tone. Format according to APA guidelines, then write your 250-word reflection: 150 words documenting specific changes made and 100 words analyzing what you learned about yourself as a writer.

Revision is the stage where the dependence trap is easiest to fall into, because the request, “improve this essay,” seems aligned with what revision is. The difference is this: when you ask AI to revise your draft, you receive a cleaner document but you do not develop the evaluative judgment that made those improvements possible. That judgment is what this assignment is designed to build. Complete Steps 1 through 3 before opening any AI tool. Your revision plan, the decisions you recorded in Steps 1 and 2, is what makes your reflection in Step 4 possible.

If AI made the revision decisions, you will have nothing genuine to document in the reflection. A growth accelerator for this assignment sounds like: “Here is my revised draft. Here is the specific change I made to my thesis. Can you tell me whether the revision is visible in the rest of the essay? Point to any paragraphs that no longer connect to the new thesis.” A dependence trap sounds like: “Revise this essay and strengthen the argument.”

Step 5: Requirements Checklist

Before you submit, verify:

  • Applied big picture revisions (thesis, organization, evidence)
  • Made paragraph-level improvements (focus, transitions, details)
  • Completed close-up editing (style, grammar, formatting)
  • Included 250-word revision reflection Used proper APA formatting and saved as PDF

Step 6: Submit the Assignment

  • Submit your assignment to W06 Writing Project in Canvas, which is due by the end of the week.

Success Criteria

Excellent work will:

  • Demonstrate substantial improvement from original draft in content, organization, and style
  • Show evidence of applying all three revision stages systematically
  • Include specific, insightful reflection on revision decisions and writing growth

Tips for Being a Successful Online Student

Focus Exclusively on the Task: If you reflect on your last study session, you likely spent time working or focusing on things other than one assignment. Our world is full of distractions. From work to social media, so many things are attempting to get our attention all the time. Finding three or four short (30-minute) distraction-free study times will help you accomplish more than several hours of back-to-back study.

Tips for Improving Your Writing

Study Grammar and Style: You do not need to know everything about grammar rules, punctuation, and sentence structure. However, as you examine feedback, you may be able to identify a pattern of mistakes that you could eliminate. An effective strategy is to start with simple rules like sentence construction or capitalization. Then move to more complex rules like commas and semicolons. This type of learning should continue long after this term ends.

AI Usage Policy

This assignment develops critical self-evaluation skills and strategic revision decision-making abilities. AI cannot know your personal writing challenges, your specific revision priorities, or your authentic growth experiences as a writer. The learning objectives focus on building metacognitive awareness and systematic revision skills that only come from genuine engagement with your own writing process.

PROHIBITED : Having AI write content, ideas, or analysis for you

  • Why prohibited: AI cannot make authentic revision decisions about your writing, evaluate your personal growth, or provide genuine insights about your writing process

ASK FIRST : Translation assistance or major editing questions

  • Why restricted: Translation support should preserve your original ideas, and editing support should not replace your revision learning

APPROVED : Grammar and spelling identification after completing your draft

  • Why allowed: Technical writing support does not compromise your revision decision-making and helps all students communicate their ideas clearly
Approved AI Prompts

Use these prompts to stay in control of your work:

  • Grammar Check: “Please identify grammar and spelling errors in this paragraph without changing my ideas: [paste your text]”
  • Clarity Review: “Which sentences in this paragraph could be clearer? Please point them out without rewriting: [paste your text]”
  • Translation Support: “Help me express this idea more clearly in English while keeping my original meaning: [paste specific sentence]”
AI Use Declaration & Attribution

At the top of your submission, include one of the following statements:

  • “I did not use AI assistance on this assignment.”
  • “[AI tool name] helped with [specific function] after I completed my draft. All ideas and content are my own work.”

Please note that you may be asked to describe how you used AI and to share transcripts from your AI conversations. These conversations with instructors are meant to help you understand the appropriate way to use AI to improve your learning.
