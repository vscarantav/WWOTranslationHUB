Chapter 4 Reading Journal

Overview

  • Task : Read the article and complete a journal entry responding to the reflection questions.
  • Purpose: To reflect on the message, make personal connections, and share insights with your peers.

Each week, we read an example of writing that uses different tactics to convey a message. The delivery of the message is just as important as the message itself.

This week, we will read " We Can Do Hard Things through Him, " by Elder Isaac K. Morrison, a special witness of Jesus Christ.

In your reading journal, please write and reflect on the following questions after you have finished reading:

  • What was the key message delivered?
  • What choices did the author make in writing?
  • When it came to the delivery of the message, what strategy did you think was most effective?
  • What could you incorporate into your writing in the future?

These reflection questions are designed to develop your ability to read as a writer, noticing the choices an author makes and evaluating their effectiveness. That analytical skill builds each week as you practice it. If you ask AI to answer these questions for you, the journal entry gets completed but the skill does not develop. Write your responses from your own reading experience. Your observations do not need to be polished or lengthy. They need to be yours. There will be no grade for this assignment, but it will help you explore a variety of approaches to writing.

The Hand of the Lord

Our Heavenly Father loves us and blesses us. His love can be seen in personal revelation, answered prayers, or miracles. However, it can also be seen in a moment of inspiration, a feeling of reassurance, or a quiet prompting. Sometimes the Hand of the Lord in our lives may be difficult to recognize and sometimes it is more clear. However, reflecting each day on how the Lord has blessed us will help us better recognize His influence in our lives.

WhatsApp Discussion

Each day, we invite you to reflect on how the Hand of the Lord was evident in your life and record your thoughts and feelings. Keeping a record of how you have felt or seen the Hand of the Lord can be a blessing for you and your family. It may also bless countless others. As you feel prompted to share how you have seen the Hand of the Lord in your life, please consider posting your experience in the course WhatsApp community. Your post should bless the lives of others and strengthen testimonies that the Lord is actively involved in our lives.
