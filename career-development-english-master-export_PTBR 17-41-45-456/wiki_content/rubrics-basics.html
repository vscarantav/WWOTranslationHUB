Rubric Basics

Nearly every discussion board and assignment in this course has a rubric. Your submissions will be graded using the rubric, so you would be wise to compare your work to the rubric before submitting an assignment or discussion board post.

Where do I find Rubrics in my course?

Discussion Board:

 

  1. Select the three dots located in the top right corner of the screen.
  2. Choose Show Rubric.

 

 

 

Assignments:

 

Find the rubric directly below the assignment instructions.

 

 

 

Where do I see rubric feedback once an assignment is graded?

 

Once the assignment is graded, you can view the grade by doing the following:

 

  • Select Grades in the left navigation bar (1).
  • Find the correct assignment (2) and select the rubric icon to the right (3).
